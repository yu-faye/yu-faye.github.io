⛓ d2bc2ff859
F-RESIDUAL
2,501 bn NOK of the 2022→2025 growth is NOT explained by returns
Compounding the reported annual returns onto the 2022 year-end value yields 18,785 bn vs reported 21,286.0 bn. The residual (+2,501 bn, +11.8% of end value) is net government flows + krone/measurement effects — facts are mutually consistent only because this residual has an identifiable cause.
F-CURRENCY
Unit of account matters: +71% in NOK is +67% in USD
USD/NOK moved 9.86 → 10.08 (Norges Bank reference rate, exhibit NB-FX). Part of the NOK-denominated growth is the measuring stick shrinking, not performance. Correct facts + wrong frame can still mislead.
F-SCOPE-2024
AR2024 states the annual return as both 2,511 bn and 2,515 bn
Same metric, same document, different scopes (AR2024 p.3, AR2024 p.14) — before/after management costs, whole fund vs investment portfolio. A single-source reader never notices; a cross-source audit flags it automatically.
F-ASSUMPTION
The 3% real-return assumption holds: realized 4.3% since 1998
The fiscal rule assumes 3.0% expected real return; the fund reports 4.3% realized annual net real return (AR2025 p.8). Assumption tested against outcome, not against authority.
